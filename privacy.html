Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Daily Impossible respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the app and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

Daily Impossible may collect basic gameplay information such as scores, streaks, leaderboard rankings, achievements, and app usage data.

If you create a profile, information such as your username, selected country, profile icon, titles, or other customization choices may also be stored.

Advertising and Analytics

Daily Impossible may use third-party advertising or analytics services. These services may collect device information, advertising identifiers, usage data, and other information according to their own privacy policies.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate the app, maintain leaderboards, track progress and streaks, improve gameplay, provide rewards, prevent abuse, and improve the overall user experience.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate features such as analytics, advertising, hosting, or leaderboards.

Children's Privacy

Daily Impossible is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.
